Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 May 2026

Sweep ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What we collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide the service and respond to enquiries:

  • Email address: when you sign up for the trial or contact us.
  • Account information: name, organisation, and the details you provide when creating an account.
  • Test and bug data: the test definitions, run results, and AI verdicts generated by your use of the platform.
  • API keys you provide: stored encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. We never log, share, or use them outside of fulfilling your test runs.
  • Technical logs: IP address, browser type, and request timestamps, retained for security and debugging.

2. How we use it

  • To operate the Sweep service: run tests, generate verdicts, and surface results.
  • To respond to your messages and support requests.
  • To improve the product through aggregate, anonymised usage analysis.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your test data, bugs, or API keys to train AI models.

3. Third-party services

To deliver the product, we transmit limited data to:

  • AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) — only the prompts required for test generation and bug verification, using the API keys you supply.
  • Infrastructure (Render) — hosts the application and database.
  • Device clouds (BrowserStack, when configured) — for mobile test execution.

4. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as you have an active account, plus a reasonable period afterwards for legal and operational reasons. You can request deletion at any time by emailing hello@sweepqa.com.

5. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the UK and EU under GDPR, and California under CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to processing or request restriction.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@sweepqa.com.

6. Security

We protect your data using industry-standard practices, including TLS in transit, AES-256-GCM encryption at rest for sensitive fields, and access controls. No system is perfectly secure; we take reasonable steps and will notify affected users of any breach as required by law.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to active users by email or in-product notice before they take effect.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@sweepqa.com.
